# Current State Complaint Journey

## Objective

Understand how complaints are handled today without the proposed assistant.

## Stage 1 — Complaint Intake

**Actor:** Customer  
**Activity:** Customer submits complaint through branch, phone, email, or online channel.

**Systems**

- Email
- CRM
- Service desk

**Pain points**

- Information often incomplete
- Complaint categories inconsistent
- Manual data entry required

**Delay:** Waiting for additional customer information.

---

## Stage 2 — Complaint Assignment

**Actor:** Complaint Team Lead  
**Activity:** Assign complaint to analyst.

**Systems**

- Case management system
- Email

**Pain points**

- Manual triage
- Inconsistent prioritization

**Handoff:** Team Lead → Complaint Analyst

---

## Stage 3 — Investigation

**Actor:** Complaint Analyst  
**Activity:** Gather information from multiple systems.

**Systems**

- Customer database
- Account systems
- Complaint repository
- Service request system

**Pain points**

- Multiple system searches
- Duplicate lookups
- Missing evidence

**Delay:** Waiting for information retrieval.

---

## Stage 4 — Policy Review

**Actor:** Complaint Analyst  
**Activity:** Search policy documents and procedures.

**Systems**

- Shared documentation repositories
- Policy knowledge base

**Pain points**

- Difficult information discovery
- Version inconsistency
- Time-consuming search

**Research:** Reported policy search.

---

## Stage 5 — Recommendation Preparation

**Actor:** Complaint Analyst  
**Activity:** Prepare recommendation.

**Systems**

- Word documents
- Email
- Case management tool

**Pain points**

- Manual summarization
- Inconsistent format
- Missing evidence references

---

## Stage 6 — Approval Review

**Actor:** Supervisor  
**Activity:** Review recommendation and evidence.

**Systems**

- Email
- Case management system

**Pain points**

- Missing information
- Supporting evidence not centralized

**Handoff:** Analyst → Supervisor  
**Result:** Returned for additional investigation.

---

## Stage 7 — Customer Response

**Actor:** Complaint Analyst  
**Activity:** Prepare final response.

**Systems**

- CRM
- Email

**Pain points**

- Rewriting explanations
- Manual evidence verification

---

## Stage 8 — Closure

**Actor:** Complaint Analyst  
**Activity:** Close complaint and archive records.

**Systems**

- Case management repository

**Pain points**

- Manual audit updates
- Missing documentation

**Outcome:** Complaint closed.
