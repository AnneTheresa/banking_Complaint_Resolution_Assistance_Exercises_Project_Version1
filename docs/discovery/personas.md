# Personas

## Persona 1 — Complaint Analyst

### Purpose

Investigate customer complaints, gather evidence, determine appropriate next steps, and prepare recommendations for review.

### Responsibilities

- Review complaint submissions
- Validate complaint details
- Collect supporting evidence
- Review customer complaint history
- Review account and service request information
- Consult approved policy guidance
- Prepare recommendation drafts
- Escalate sensitive complaints
- Maintain accurate records

### Primary Goals

- Resolve complaints accurately
- Reduce investigation time
- Ensure recommendations are evidence-based
- Maintain regulatory compliance
- Improve customer experience

### Key Tasks

1. Review incoming complaint
2. Identify complaint category
3. Determine urgency
4. Locate supporting records
5. Investigate relevant customer history
6. Review applicable policies
7. Draft recommendation
8. Submit for approval when required

### Information Needs

- Customer information
- Account information
- Historical complaints
- Service requests
- Policies and procedures
- Escalation criteria
- Audit history

### Permissions

- View complaint records
- View customer complaint history
- View service requests
- Create recommendation drafts
- Request escalation

### Constraints

- Cannot approve sensitive resolutions
- Cannot alter records without authorization
- Must document supporting evidence
- Must follow approved policies

### Success Measures

- Accurate categorization
- Evidence-supported recommendations
- Reduced investigation effort
- Complete audit trail

## Persona 2 — Supervisor / Reviewer

### Purpose

Review sensitive complaints and provide final approval decisions.

### Responsibilities

- Review analyst recommendations
- Validate evidence quality
- Ensure policy compliance
- Approve or reject recommendations
- Authorize sensitive outcomes
- Monitor complaint quality

### Primary Goals

- Protect customers and organization
- Ensure fair outcomes
- Ensure compliance requirements are met
- Reduce decision risk

### Key Tasks

1. Review escalation requests
2. Review recommendation draft
3. Review supporting evidence
4. Approve, reject, or request changes
5. Record decision rationale

### Information Needs

- Complaint details
- Recommendation draft
- Supporting evidence
- Policy references
- Escalation history
- Audit information

### Permissions

- View all complaint records
- Approve resolutions
- Reject resolutions
- Request additional investigation

### Constraints

- Must justify decisions
- Must follow policy guidance
- Cannot approve unsupported recommendations
- Must preserve auditability

### Success Measures

- Consistent decision quality
- Compliance adherence
- Reduced risk exposure
- Complete audit records
